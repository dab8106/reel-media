Privacy Policy

Dabeer AI Tool is a private tool that Dabeer Shaikh uses to run his own Instagram account, @dabeer.ai, and its linked Facebook Page. It is not offered to other businesses or the public.

What data the tool handles

  • Our own account: posts, captions, publishing status and insights (views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, watch time) for @dabeer.ai.
  • Comments on our posts: the comment text, the commenter's Instagram username and comment ID, so we can reply and, when someone comments a keyword we asked for, send them the one direct message they requested.
  • Direct messages: only the messages the tool sends in response to such a comment. It does not read people's inboxes.
  • Public information about other professional accounts and hashtags: public profile counts and public posts (captions, like and comment counts, links), used to understand which topics and formats work in our niche.

How the data is used

Only to publish and schedule our content, reply to comments, send the requested DM, and measure and improve our content. We do not sell, rent or share this data, use it for advertising, build profiles of individuals, or contact anyone who did not ask to be contacted.

Where it is stored and for how long

Analytics snapshots are stored in a private GitHub repository and on the owner's computer. Comment IDs of people we have messaged are kept so nobody is messaged twice; they are deleted after 90 days. Public research snapshots (other professional accounts and hashtags) are kept only while useful for our content planning, and any of them can be deleted on request. Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and never published.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any data about you at any time; see Data deletion. You can also remove a comment, block @dabeer.ai, or ignore a DM; we never send more than one message per comment.

Meta platform data

Data from Instagram and Facebook is used in line with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.
